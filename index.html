Virtual strategy limits: £100 starting cash, up to £100 for one exceptionally well-qualified research setup (smaller £40/£70 levels for weaker evidence), no more than two open positions, at most one new entry per New York trading day, and never more than available virtual cash. Position exits can be triggered by an illustrative 2.5% stop, 5% target or a 65-minute time limit. New entries stop when fewer than 105 minutes remain in the session, and a close is attempted at least 30 minutes before the US market shuts. The ledger pauses new entries when marked equity is £80 or lower; that floor is not a guarantee against larger losses. Currency uses published indicative ECB reference rates, not a broker conversion quote. The simulator checks exits every five minutes. If a fresh price or data service is unavailable near closing, a virtual holding can still remain open; this is flagged and scheduled for a next-session recovery exit, never a deliberate swing trade. Simulated spread/slippage and stop prices are not real fills.
